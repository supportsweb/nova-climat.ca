Gree heat pumps

Gree ductless and central heat pumps are recognized for their energy efficiency, reliability and ability to operate effectively even in extremely cold temperatures.

Clivia series

These ductless heat pumps are designed to maintain excellent heating performance even when outdoor temperatures drop to -30°C, while delivering exceptional energy efficiency.

The models feature Inverter technology, which automatically adjusts output to maintain a consistent indoor temperature while reducing electricity consumption.

Charmo series

Depending on the model, the Charmo series can efficiently heat a home in temperatures ranging from approximately -25°C to -30°C while delivering excellent energy performance.

These ductless heat pumps include dehumidification, self-cleaning and built-in Wi-Fi control.

Airy series

These compact ductless heat pumps are discreet and exceptionally quiet. They provide efficient heating down to approximately -25°C to -30°C and economical cooling throughout the summer.

FLEXX central heat pump

The FLEXX central heat pump is designed to heat and cool homes and commercial buildings by distributing warm or cool air throughout multiple rooms using a duct system.

Its Inverter technology automatically adjusts operating capacity to maintain a stable indoor temperature, maximizing comfort while reducing energy consumption.

Slim-B central heat pump

This ducted system provides even heating and cooling throughout the entire home. It is also an excellent choice for commercial buildings, as it can efficiently heat and cool large spaces.

This quiet heat pump delivers outstanding energy efficiency, helping you significantly reduce your heating costs.

Elite B series

These premium central heat pumps deliver exceptional performance in extremely cold weather.

Multi B Series

LG Heat Pumps

LG residential heat pumps are recognized for their Inverter technology, quiet operation and smart control features.

LG models automatically adjust their output according to demand, provide excellent heating performance down to approximately -25°C to -30°C, offer remote control capabilities and operate quietly.

LG DualCool

This high-performance, energy-efficient and quiet ductless heat pump is an excellent choice for homes, cottages and condominiums.

LG Art Cool

LG Multi F

Hitachi heat pumps

Hitachi air-to-water heat pumps use thermal energy from the outdoor air to provide heating down to -20°C, air conditioning and domestic hot water without requiring an indoor hydraulic module.

On colder days, an integrated electric backup heating system automatically activates to ensure reliable and efficient heating.

Elios heat pumps

The Elios central heat pump combines outstanding energy efficiency with advanced technologies such as Heat+ and a variable-speed compressor, ultra-quiet operation (58 dB) and smart controls.

It provides heating down to -30°C and delivers cooling efficiency ratings of up to 18.3 SEER2.
