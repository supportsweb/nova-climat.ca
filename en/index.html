Financial assistance

Financial assistance programs available

You may qualify for one of the following financial assistance programs (as a homeowner or tenant) to help cover the purchase and installation of a ductless or central heat pump.

LogisVert

The LogisVert program reimburses part of the purchase and installation cost of a heat pump designed to perform efficiently in very cold weather. The amount varies depending on the model and can reach several thousand dollars.

View the program

Éconologis

The Éconologis program is available to eligible homeowners and tenants who wish to receive a free ductless heat pump, including installation at no cost.

View the program

Chauffez vert

Chauffez Vert provides financial assistance to replace a central heating system powered by oil or propane with a new heat pump.

View the program
