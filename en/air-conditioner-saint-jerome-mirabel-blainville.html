Financial Assistance

Financial Assistance eligibility

To qualify for financial assistance available to eligible homeowners and tenants, the selected model must deliver high performance in extremely cold temperatures. Refer to Hydro-Québec's official list of eligible models.

Some eligible models include the following.

Gree, LG and Hitachi

Several cold-climate product lines, including the Gree Extreme, LG ART COOL and Hitachi airHome 800 heat pumps, qualify for rebates of up to $120 or $140 per 1,000 BTU at -8°C, depending on the program requirements.

Elios

Several Heat+ models, including selected ductless, multi-zone and central heat pumps, are eligible for available financial assistance programs.

Stelpro

The Elite B, Pro B and Multi B series, along with several Stelpro central heat pump models, qualify for government financial assistance programs.
