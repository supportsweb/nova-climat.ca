How do you choose the right air conditioning system?

Selecting the right model depends on several factors, including the area to be cooled, the number of rooms, the desired noise level and your budget.

Cooling performance is indicated by the SEER2 rating, which measures seasonal energy efficiency in cooling mode.

Cooling capacity

BTU ratings indicate the amount of heat a heat pump can remove from your home in one hour. A 12,000 BTU/h unit is suitable for smaller spaces, while a 24,000 BTU/h unit can efficiently heat and cool a much larger area.

Central heat pumps are typically available in capacities of 36,000, 48,000 or 60,000 BTU/h, depending on the size of the home.

Quiet models

For bedrooms and smaller spaces, LG ductless air conditioning systems in 9,000 and 12,000 BTU capacities are among the quietest available, operating at just 17 dB.

Hitachi also offers exceptionally quiet systems with its airHome series, available from 9,000 to 24,000 BTU, producing as little as 19 dB for quiet comfort in rooms of all sizes.

Gree manufactures high-performance systems ranging from 9,000 to 24,000 BTU that operate at approximately 22 dB on low fan speed, with higher sound levels at maximum airflow.

Elios and Stelpro also offer systems ranging from 9,000 to 24,000 BTU. Their 12,000 BTU models typically operate between 22 dB and 23 dB, making them ideal for living areas, although they may be slightly more noticeable in bedrooms during the night.

A professional assessment by one of our specialists will help determine the system that best matches your needs.

Manufacturer warranties

Warranty coverage varies depending on the manufacturer.

Gree offers a limited warranty of 10 to 12 years on parts and the compressor when the unit is registered.

LG, Elios and Hitachi provide a 10-year warranty on the Inverter compressor and a 5-year warranty on parts.

Stelpro offers a limited warranty of either 1 year or 5 years, depending on the model, while maintaining an affordable purchase price.
