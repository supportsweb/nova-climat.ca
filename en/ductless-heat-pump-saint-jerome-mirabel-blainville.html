Performance & technology

Gree ductless heat pumps

Gree Charmo Series ductless heat pumps deliver excellent energy efficiency, with ratings of up to 22 SEER2 for cooling and 9 HSPF2 for heating, depending on the model.

They are available in capacities ranging from 9,000 to 36,000 BTU, making them suitable for heating and cooling anything from a single room to an entire home.

These quiet systems offer outstanding value while delivering exceptional performance throughout both summer and winter. Whether you need efficient cooling or reliable heating in temperatures as low as -25°C, they are designed for Québec's northern climate.

Thanks to their variable-speed Inverter compressor, these ductless heat pumps automatically adjust their output to maintain your desired indoor temperature. Because the compressor does not constantly stop and restart, electricity consumption is reduced while indoor comfort remains consistent.

In cooling mode, the built-in dehumidification function removes excess moisture from the air and expels it outdoors.

In addition, the self-cleaning function helps reduce the buildup of mold and bacteria inside the system, improving indoor air quality.

Built-in Wi-Fi allows you to control your home's temperature remotely using your smartphone.
