When should a heat pump Be repaired?

When should a heat pump Be repaired?

If your heat pump no longer heats properly, cools less efficiently or starts making unusual noises, it is time to have it repaired.

One of our heat pump technicians will come to your home or business to diagnose and resolve the problem.

Prompt repairs often prevent more serious breakdowns and help extend the lifespan of your system.

We service ductless and central heat pumps from most major manufacturers to restore their performance and efficiency.

What are the signs of a faulty heat pump?

Several symptoms may indicate that your heat pump requires repair, including reduced performance, failure to start, weak airflow, error codes, water leaks, frequent defrost cycles or unusual noises.

A heat pump that runs continuously without reaching the desired temperature or causes a noticeable increase in your electricity bill may also require professional attention.

Technical inspection

Inspection and diagnosis

Before replacing any components, our technician performs a complete inspection of your system to determine the exact cause of the problem, whether mechanical or electrical.

The inspection includes the compressor, fans, electronic control boards, sensors, electrical connections, filters, coils and refrigerant level.

This thorough diagnosis allows us to recommend the appropriate repair while avoiding unnecessary part replacements.

Targeted repair

Repairs

Depending on the issue, we may replace components such as the fan or fan motor, capacitor, electronic control board, sensors, relays, blower motor, defrost components, control devices and electrical connections.

If all major components appear to be in good condition, our technician will also check for clogged filters, dirty coils or fan performance issues, as these can significantly reduce the efficiency of your heat pump.

Prompt repair

Why repair your heat pump promptly?

Ignoring a heat pump problem can lead to premature wear of important components and result in higher repair costs.

Addressing the issue as soon as possible often limits damage and helps maintain optimal energy efficiency.

Our goal is to restore your heat pump to proper working condition as quickly as possible.
