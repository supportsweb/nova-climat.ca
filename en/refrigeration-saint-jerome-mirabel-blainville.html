Diagnosis & repair

Common refrigeration equipment repairs

Compressor

Compressor

A compressor may fail to start, become noisy or operate intermittently. When this occurs, the refrigeration system is no longer able to maintain the required temperature.

Our technician will inspect the electrical supply, starting relays and capacitors. If the compressor is overheating, seized or has lost its performance, it will need to be replaced.

Once the repair is completed, the refrigeration system is evacuated, placed under vacuum and recharged with refrigerant.

Refrigerant leaks

A refrigerator or freezer loses efficiency when refrigerant leaks occur. Temperatures become unstable and the equipment struggles to maintain proper cooling.

Our technicians locate refrigerant leaks using specialized tools such as electronic leak detectors and nitrogen pressure testing.

The damaged section is then repaired by brazing the copper refrigerant lines, replacing damaged tubing or installing new components as required.

Finally, we perform a leak test to verify the integrity of the system before recharging it with refrigerant according to the manufacturer's specifications.

Thermostats and sensors

When thermostats or temperature sensors begin to fail, temperatures become unstable and the refrigeration system may no longer operate properly.

Our certified refrigeration technician tests electrical continuity and resistance values to confirm the source of the problem. The faulty component is then replaced, and the temperature control system is tested to ensure proper operation.

Fans

The indoor evaporator fan and the outdoor condenser fan can both develop problems. When they stop working or operate improperly, cold air is no longer distributed efficiently.

Our technician checks whether the fans are obstructed, worn or whether the motor has failed. In some cases, a thorough cleaning is sufficient. Otherwise, the motor or the complete fan assembly is replaced.

Defrost system

When the defrost system fails, ice accumulates and blocks critical components, reducing the efficiency of the refrigeration system.

Our technician tests each component individually, including the heating element, timer, thermistor and control board. The defective part is replaced, and the system is tested to ensure ice no longer accumulates and the defrost cycle operates correctly.

Dirty condensers

When regular maintenance is neglected, the refrigeration system must work harder to maintain the required temperature.

A thorough condenser cleaning restores proper heat transfer, improves system efficiency and reduces energy consumption.

Electronic control boards

A faulty control board can cause system failures, unusual operating behaviour or prevent the equipment from starting altogether.

Our technician begins with a complete diagnosis using error codes and voltage testing. Depending on the results, the control board or individual electronic components are replaced, after which the system is reprogrammed or reset as required.
