Heat pump maintenance process

Our certified refrigeration technician has the specialized equipment required to properly maintain your ductless or central heat pump.

Coil disinfection

Our technician applies an antibacterial and antifungal treatment to the indoor coil to eliminate invisible mold and other biological contaminants.

Pressure cleaning

Using a waterproof protective cleaning bag, the indoor blower fan is pressure-cleaned to remove built-up dust and debris.

Electrical and mechanical inspection

Our technician measures refrigerant pressures, inspects electrical connections and verifies the proper operation of the temperature sensors.

The compressor's electrical amperage is also checked to ensure it is operating correctly.

Regular maintenance

What you can do regularly

To keep air circulating properly and prevent unnecessary strain on the motor, we recommend the following maintenance tasks.

Clean the filters

Every four to six weeks during the cooling season, remove the filters from the indoor unit, vacuum away accumulated dust and wash them with warm water and mild soap.

Allow the filters to air dry completely before reinstalling them. Dirty filters reduce system efficiency and negatively affect indoor air quality.

Keep the outdoor unit clear

Maintain at least two feet of clear space around the outdoor unit.

During the summer, remove grass clippings, leaves and pollen that may block the outdoor coil.

During the winter, regularly remove snow from the top and sides of the unit and clear away any ice that could interfere with the fan blades.

Check the condensate drain

Ensure that condensation drains properly outdoors while the system is operating in cooling mode to prevent water leaks inside your home.
