Installation process

Planning

Before installing the ventilation ductwork, we evaluate the size and layout of your home or business to determine the proper duct dimensions and routing.

Our technician records the number of rooms as well as the location of walls and ceilings to determine the most efficient duct layout and sizing.

Installation

Properly sized ductwork is strategically installed to optimize airflow while reducing electricity consumption.

We install rigid galvanized steel ductwork, insulating it where required, along with all necessary fittings, plenums, dampers and supply registers.

Every component is installed according to industry standards to ensure an airtight system while minimizing air leakage and condensation.

Air outlets are evenly distributed throughout the building to prevent temperature differences between rooms. A well-designed duct system helps maintain a consistent indoor temperature in both heating and cooling modes.

We then install the outdoor heat pump unit, the indoor air handler and the control wiring.

Ductwork adaptation Existing duct system

Ductwork adaptation

When an existing ventilation duct system is already in place and in good condition, we can adapt it for the installation of a new central heat pump.

We make all necessary modifications to ensure the entire system operates efficiently.

Once the heat pump is installed and all connections are completed, we verify the performance of the duct system, airflow and overall operation to ensure everything functions as intended.

The importance of proper installation

Poorly installed ventilation ductwork can lead to several problems, including inadequate airflow, energy loss, excessive ventilation noise, premature heat pump wear and uneven temperatures throughout the building.

For this reason, every installation must be completed with precision and in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations.

We place great importance on installation quality, secure mounting and airtight connections throughout the entire system.
