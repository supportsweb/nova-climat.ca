Elios central heat pumps

The Elios central heat pump incorporates the latest technologies to provide reliable heating during the winter and efficient cooling throughout the summer, with capacities of up to 60,000 BTU.

It delivers dependable heating during long winters in temperatures as low as -30°C and performs exceptionally well during periods of extreme summer heat.

In addition, it operates very quietly and is up to 40% more compact than many other central heat pump models.

Energy efficiency

Outstanding Performance

Elios central heat pumps are among the most efficient and energy-saving systems available on the market. Their performance and efficiency are recognized through ENERGY STAR® and NEEP certifications.

Their excellent coefficient of performance (COP) can exceed 3 under certain outdoor conditions, meaning the system can produce more than 3 kWh of heat while consuming only 1 kWh of electricity.

As a result, heating costs can be three to four times lower than with electric baseboard heaters.

Thanks to Inverter technology, the compressor continuously adjusts its output to maintain your desired indoor temperature for both heating and cooling. This eliminates frequent start-and-stop cycles, further reducing electricity consumption while improving overall comfort.
