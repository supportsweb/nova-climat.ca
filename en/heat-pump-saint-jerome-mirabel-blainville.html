Performance & comfort

Benefits of heat pumps

High-efficiency operation

Unlike systems that generate heat, such as electric baseboards, heat pumps extract thermal energy from the outdoor air and transfer it indoors to heat your home.

Electric baseboards convert 1 kWh of electricity into 1 kWh of heat, while ductless and central heat pumps produce between two and four times more heating energy than the electricity they consume.

Outstanding energy savings

Thanks to their exceptional efficiency, heat pumps can significantly reduce your electricity bills, with savings of up to 40% for a well-insulated average-sized home.

Their Inverter compressor continuously adjusts output to maintain a stable indoor temperature without constantly stopping and restarting, further improving efficiency.

Heating down to -30°C

Cold-climate heat pumps continue to provide efficient heating even when outdoor temperatures drop as low as -30°C.

Cost-effective cooling

A heat pump also functions as an air conditioner and dehumidifier during the summer, eliminating the need to purchase and maintain two separate systems.

It also cools your home far more efficiently than a portable or window air conditioner.

Comfort

Central heat pumps distribute conditioned air evenly throughout your home using ductwork, while ductless systems provide precise temperature control in the areas where you need it most.

Improved indoor air quality and dehumidification

These advanced systems filter dust and reduce indoor humidity, creating cleaner, healthier and more comfortable indoor air.
